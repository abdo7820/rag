LiverGuard
Hepatology Evidence Engine
Single-source · fully cited · self-verifying

Ask a research paper anything, get back only what it actually says.

LiverGuard reads one hepatology paper — Liver diseases: epidemiology, causes, trends and predictions — through semantic search, BM25 and a knowledge graph, fuses the candidates with RRF, reranks with a cross-encoder, then drafts and re-verifies the answer before a single citation is allowed to reach you. Nothing ships without a [Source: section, p.N] tag traceable back to the text.
